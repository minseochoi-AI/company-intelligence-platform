# Configure → baseline → gap → augment → decide

## CONFIGURE

Persist before research:

- founder resources: programming, server access, grants/scholarships, partner access;
- limits: weekly hours, cash, geography, runway, hardware prototype budget, certifications, field-test access;
- desired barrier: physical integration, proprietary/rights-cleared data flywheel, algorithmic know-how, regulated workflow, or distribution;
- forbidden paths: direct head-on competition, data without product-use rights, grant-only demand, unsafe automation;
- comparison axes shared with career planning.

Unknown values are explicit `null`, not defaults. Keep `research_gaps` exactly equal to the validator-calculated missing constraint paths; store broader market questions in hypothesis `unresolved` fields.

## BASELINE and GAP

For each sector and hypothesis capture a source ledger, buyer/user/payer split, market formula, competitor/substitute matrix, adoption path, public-data feasibility, rights, physical deployment, algorithmic novelty, and subsidy/grant fit. Then use the validator's `gaps` command. Research the highest-decision-impact gaps only.

## AUGMENT and RECLASSIFY

Append evidence with observed time and content hash. Never overwrite an inconvenient source; mark claims conflicted. Recompute the market archetype after every material competitor or buyer finding. Version each hypothesis revision.

## DECIDE

Use gates before weighted scoring:

1. legal/ethical and data-use viability;
2. reachable buyer and paid problem;
3. feasible first pilot within cash/time/field-access limits;
4. evidence-backed differentiation and barrier path;
5. downside and kill experiment.

Only survivors may be compared. Report sensitivity to weights, unresolved facts, next experiment, and kill criteria. Do not allow grant eligibility to rescue a failed demand gate.
