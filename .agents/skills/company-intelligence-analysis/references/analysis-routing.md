# Analysis routing

Choose by question, then inspect the command's current help.

| Question | CIL route | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Source readiness | `원천-상태` | Catalog eligibility is not live success. |
| Entity identity, ownership, legal form | `기업-기관-검색` | Unresolved identity remains unknown. |
| Comparable company metrics | `기업-비교` or `선별` | Match country, concept, unit, currency, period, vintage, and coverage. |
| Time-ordered company evidence | `기업-시간축` | Enforce as-of availability; avoid hindsight. |
| Explain or reproduce a result | `근거-설명`, `선택-재현` | Verify saved input and result hashes. |
| Portable tables | `증거-내보내기` | Review redaction and manifest before sharing. |

Use `company-intel 사용-안내 --명령 <명령>` and `<명령> --도움말` rather than memorizing options. Current job searches belong to the career workflow, even though CIL supplies their evidence.
